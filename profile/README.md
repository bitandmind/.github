### Why "Bit & Mind"

In 1948, Claude Shannon's *A Mathematical Theory of Communication* put the word
**bit** into print and turned information into something we can measure.
Much of modern computing, from compression to networks to language models,
still stands on that paper.

Bit & Mind is our small tribute to that idea: the **bit** is what machines measure,
the **mind** is what turns it into meaning.

### The mark

Our logo is Shannon's binary entropy curve, H(p). It peaks at p = 0.5, where
uncertainty is highest and a single answer carries exactly **1 bit**.
The dot floating above the peak is that bit.

### How we work

- **Signal over noise**: ship what matters, cut the rest.
- **Say it in fewer bits**: simple code, short docs, clear names.
- **Respect the uncertainty**: measure before deciding, and when it's 50/50, say so.
